ExtraBiomes: size, placement and density vs vanilla

Default config, Overworld · 25 surface biomes + Deep Dark Green (underground) · simulated over 12,885 km² of terrain (3 seeds, vanilla multi-noise)

10.1%of the world's surface is an ExtraBiomes biome at default weights (sum of all 25)
0.22%median mod biome share. Vanilla's rarest land biomes: Windswept Gravelly Hills 0.09%, Stony Peaks 0.10%, Pale Garden 0.14%, Jagged Peaks 0.15%, Frozen Peaks 0.16%, Eroded Badlands 0.19%, Windswept Savanna 0.20%, Ice Spikes 0.21%
2 of 25biomes are effectively absent (Floating Jungle, Charred Forest), under 0.015% of the world
1/6of the world is pure vanilla (TerraBlender vanilla region), the other 5/6 can host mod biomes

Findings

Floating Jungle and Charred Forest are practically unreachable

Floating Jungle needs warm + humid + mid/far inland + erosion 0–1 + PEAK_NORMAL weirdness: only 0.007% of the world, roughly one visible patch per ~197 km. Charred Forest (0.012%) is a single-bucket near-inland continentalness band on top of a narrow weirdness slice, about one patch per ~66 km. Widening Floating Jungle's weirdness to PEAK_NORMAL+HIGH_SLICE_NORMAL_DESCENDING or Charred Forest's continentalness to NEAR_INLAND→MID_INLAND would fix this.

The "Rare" region does not make its biomes rare

Mystic Forest has the lowest Bedrock frequency (amount 0.05) yet is the second-largest mod biome at 1.17%, about 11× its Bedrock-intended share. Its box (neutral temp, wet→humid, any inland, erosion 0–4, all weirdness) is huge, so the weight-10 region only trims it by half. Jellyfish Fields (0.94%) and The Netherlands Mutated (0.50%) are bigger than most primary-region biomes. Region weight is a weaker lever than box size, so rarity tiers need box tuning, not just weights.

Several biomes the Bedrock pack makes common are scarce on Java

Measured against Bedrock replace_biomes.amount × the vanilla biomes it replaces, the Java versions of Moorlands (2% of intended), Jungle Marsh (7%), Glacier (7%), Fungle Jungle (9%), The Netherlands (8%) and Taiga Spikes (21%) occupy a small fraction of the area Bedrock gives them. Most have small climate boxes (Moorlands, for example, is limited to erosion 5–6). Moorlands also had its box moved into the Rare region, which halves its weight again.

Cold Mesa, Tropical Island and Grand Oasis land close to Bedrock's intent

Their world share is within ~15% of the Bedrock-intended figure (Lush Mesa is at 54%, Jungle Pillars and Desert Bryce around 75%). Cold Mesa is also the one mod biome with vanilla-scale or larger patches (area-weighted median ≈ 1577 blocks across, vs ~1200 for vanilla Desert).

Patch size: most mod biomes are small, spiky pockets

Typical (area-weighted median) patch widths are 242–1014 blocks, with Cold Mesa the outlier at 1577. Vanilla Forest/Plains patches are ~529–628 blocks and vanilla oceans/deserts 800–1000+. Taiga Spikes, Shattered Swamp and Tropical Island are the smallest (242, 248, 413 blocks). Fine for spike/island biomes, but you may travel far to see a full one.

Density: most reachable biomes show up every 5–13 km

After region masking, visible patches (≥ 0.05 km²) of each reachable biome occur every ~5–13 km.

Deep Dark Green replaces only ~1/6 of deep dark

It claims half of the weirdness range but only in the Secondary region (1/3 of the world), so ≈ 17% of deep-dark cells against Bedrock's 0.5.

Overlapping boxes (first-listed wins)

Within a region a few boxes still overlap (Cold Mesa / Taiga Spikes, Grand Oasis / Lush Mesa, Fungle Jungle / Tropical Island, Glacier / Cold Mesa Bryce / Cold Mesa Plateau / Volcanic Moss Tundra, Mystic Forest / The Netherlands). The overlaps are tiny edge slivers, so they cost little area, but the winner depends on TerraBlender's tie-break rather than your intent.

Recommendations

Each one is checked against the same simulation (box changes only, regions unchanged) and reasoned from the README description and the Bedrock behaviour. "Now → sim" is world share. Treat Bedrock's "intended" figure as a ceiling: for biomes whose Bedrock targets include forest or plains it is inflated, so 30–50% of it is a sensible goal.

(1) Apply the Moorlands, Netherlands and Floating Jungle box changes. (2) Move Moorlands and Jungle Marsh to a full-weight region, then run BiomeGenerationGameTests across seeds and check reachability. (3) Fix the overlaps those changes introduce (Moorlands/Mutated at erosion 4, Glacier/Volcanic Moss, Charred/Future Desert). (4) Re-run this report. (5) Update the Region javadocs, which currently describe the old boxes, and the beta 11 changelog.

What I couldn't tell from the README

Nothing says how common each biome is meant to be on Java, so I used Bedrock's replace_biomes as the target. If you want the quirky biomes (Future Desert, Floating Jungle, Charred Forest) kept genuinely rare, that overrides these numbers.

Method and caveats

This is a simulation, not a live world scan. The five climate noises (temperature, humidity, continentalness, erosion, weirdness) use vanilla's real noise parameters and the real NormalNoise class, sampled every 32 blocks over 3 seeds × 65 km × 65 km. Vanilla biomes are classified from the actual OverworldBiomeBuilder surface points (Minecraft 1.21.5 jar from the local Gradle cache). The mod boxes are parsed straight from ModOverworldRegion*.java and ParameterUtils bucket values. In each TerraBlender region a mod box wins where it overlaps; elsewhere vanilla fills in.

Assumptions to check against a real world: (1) TerraBlender's default vanilla-region weight is 10 and regions are chosen uniformly per cell in proportion to weight; (2) boxes are used as declared, so the extra boundary points TerraBlender's adjacency pass synthesizes aren't modelled (your Region javadocs note this changes reachability); (3) region cells are assumed larger than a biome patch, so patch sizes are not cut by region borders (spacing does include region share); (4) only surface depth is simulated, and the project targets MC 26.3 while the vanilla table comes from 1.21.5. The numbers are best read as relative comparisons. For ground truth, BiomeGenerationGameTests can count biomes in a live world.

Region weights come from Config.java: biomeWeight 20, secondaryBiomeWeight 20, rareBiomeWeight 10.
