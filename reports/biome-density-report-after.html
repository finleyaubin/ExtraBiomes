Method and caveats

This is a simulation, not a live world scan. The five climate noises (temperature, humidity, continentalness, erosion, weirdness) use vanilla's real noise parameters and the real NormalNoise class, sampled every 32 blocks over 3 seeds × 65 km × 65 km. Vanilla biomes are classified from the actual OverworldBiomeBuilder surface points (Minecraft 1.21.5 jar from the local Gradle cache). The mod boxes are parsed straight from ModOverworldRegion*.java and ParameterUtils bucket values. In each TerraBlender region a mod box wins where it overlaps; elsewhere vanilla fills in.

Assumptions to check against a real world: (1) TerraBlender's default vanilla-region weight is 10 and regions are chosen uniformly per cell in proportion to weight; (2) boxes are used as declared, so the extra boundary points TerraBlender's adjacency pass synthesizes aren't modelled (your Region javadocs note this changes reachability); (3) region cells are assumed larger than a biome patch, so patch sizes are not cut by region borders (spacing does include region share); (4) only surface depth is simulated, and the project targets MC 26.3 while the vanilla table comes from 1.21.5. The numbers are best read as relative comparisons. For ground truth, BiomeGenerationGameTests can count biomes in a live world.

Region weights come from Config.java: biomeWeight 20, secondaryBiomeWeight 20, rareBiomeWeight 10.
