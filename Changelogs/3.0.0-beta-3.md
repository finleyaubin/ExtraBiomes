### Sky City
- Sky City now only spawns above hills or mountains, rather than anywhere in the sky.
- Fountain water now flows properly down to ground level, with cave vines added underneath to break it up.
- The city's spawn point now always starts at the fountain, guaranteeing it's reachable.
- Fixed the sky island start point not working.
- Islands are bigger; clouds now follow the paths more closely, and new path shapes were added.

### World Generation
- Fixed tree cut-offs and flowerpots.
- Updated the Netherlands grass and colors (the original reddish "nether-like" look wasn't working, so it's been reworked).

### Behind the Scenes
- CI/CD pipeline now automatically uploads builds to CurseForge, with beta handling for version numbers.
- Updated `.gitignore` and switched the CurseForge upload endpoint.
