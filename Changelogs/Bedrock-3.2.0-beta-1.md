# ExtraBiomes 3.2.0 Beta 1 for Bedrock
This beta brings the Java 3.20.0 Beta 1 changes to Bedrock: the new Larch Forest and its wood set, wooden shelves, a reworked Desert Bryce and higher contrast Sky wood textures.

# Changes
## Biomes
### Larch Forest
- New biome: the Larch Forest, found on the cold mountain slopes where Groves would normally generate.
- Tall, normal and young larches with a pointed top and drooping branch tips, mixed with spruce, pine and young conifers, with open meadow clearings.
- Larch leaves come in nine shades from autumn gold to summer green, so a forest blends between colours.
- Dry grass, patches of snow and podzol, mossy rocks, big granite, diorite and andesite boulders and small kettle lakes.

### Desert Bryce
- The pillars are now smooth sandstone with bands of sandstone, the ground is marbled with packed mud and sandstone, and short and tall pointed dripstone stalagmites grow out of the packed mud.
- Added a dusty fog.

## Blocks
### Larch Wood
- A full larch wood set: logs, wood, stripped logs and wood, planks, stairs, slabs, fences, fence gates, doors, trapdoors, boats, chest boats, leaves and a sapling that grows with bone meal. Bedrock's other wood sets have no buttons, pressure plates or signs, so larch doesn't either.

### Shelves
- Added wooden shelves for Mystic, Sky, Palm, Gilded Sky and Larch wood, crafted from stripped logs. Each shelf holds up to three stacks of items. Click a slot to swap it with the item in your hand, and breaking the shelf drops what's on it. Redstone hotbar swapping isn't in yet.

### Textures
- Sky and Gilded Sky wood textures have more contrast, matching Java.

## Languages
- Larch wood and shelf names are translated into every language.
